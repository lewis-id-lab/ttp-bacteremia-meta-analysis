Overview

This appendix accompanies the main manuscript and gives (1) details of the included studies, (2) model specification, fit, diagnostics, and convergence, (3) sensitivity analyses, and (4) the software and R packages used. All numbers are generated from the extraction workbook (data/TTP_MetaAnalysis_Extraction_Complete_v4.xlsx) and the cached brms fits in the project repository.

The primary pool comprises 100 studies meeting the strict eligibility criterion (a quantitative association between TTP and a clinical outcome); 58 of these reported mortality, contributing 72 effect sizes. The sensitivity pool adds studies that met only the inclusive criterion (TTP and a clinical outcome both reported in the same cohort, not necessarily cross-tabulated); it comprises 140 studies in total, 67 of which contribute 84 mortality effect sizes.

2. Model specification

All pooled and moderator models were Bayesian random-effects models fitted with brms: \(y_i \mid \mathrm{SE}_i \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu + u_s,\ \mathrm{SE}_i^2)\) with study-level random intercepts \(u_s \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \tau^2)\), where \(y_i\) is the log odds ratio for effect size \(i\) and \(\mathrm{SE}_i\) its standard error. Priors were \(\mu \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)\), \(\tau \sim \text{Half-Cauchy}(0, 0.5)\), and \(\mathcal{N}(0, 0.5)\) for moderator coefficients. Each model used 4 chains of 4,000 iterations (1,000 warm-up; 12,000 post-warm-up draws), adapt_delta = 0.99, and max_treedepth = 12. The persistent bacteremia and microbiological clearance models were fitted with the CmdStan backend and all others with rstan. The 95% prediction interval for a new study is the central 95% interval of the predictive distribution \(\mathcal{N}(\mu, \tau^2)\). \(I^2\) was computed per posterior draw as \(\tau^2 / (\tau^2 + \bar{v})\), where \(\bar{v}\) is the mean within-study variance. Code is in R/02_fit_models.R and the analysis notebooks.

#| label: tbl-pooled-fit
#| tbl-cap: "Posterior summaries of the pooled random-effects models. OR is the posterior median odds ratio (short versus long TTP) with its 95% credible interval; PI is the 95% prediction interval for a new study; tau is the between-study SD of the log-OR; I2 is the posterior median; P(OR > 1) is the posterior probability of an association in the direction of harm."
pooled_row <- function(name, label, dir = prim_dir) {
  if (!file.exists(fit_path(name, dir))) return(NULL)
  f <- read_fit(name, dir); p <- pooled_summary(f)
  tibble(Model = label,
         k = nrow(f$data), Studies = n_distinct(f$data$study_id),
         OR = sprintf("%.2f (%.2f to %.2f)", p$median_OR, p$ci_OR[1], p$ci_OR[2]),
         PI = sprintf("%.2f to %.2f", p$pi_OR[1], p$pi_OR[2]),
         tau = sprintf("%.2f", p$median_tau),
         I2 = sprintf("%.0f%%", p$median_i2),
         `P(OR > 1)` = sprintf("%.1f%%", 100 * p$p_harm))
}
